# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a small, focused companion to a book. We want interactions here — issues, pull
requests, and discussion — to be useful, respectful, and welcoming to people of all backgrounds and
experience levels.

**Expected:** be considerate and constructive; assume good faith; keep feedback about the code and
the ideas, not the person; help newcomers.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning comments, or sharing
others' private information without consent.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, and any project communication) and to
public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported privately to **conduct@acuity.press**. Reports will be reviewed and
handled discreetly. The maintainer may remove comments, reject contributions, or block accounts
that violate this standard.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
